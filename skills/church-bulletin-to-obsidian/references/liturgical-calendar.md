# Liturgical calendar context

### Step 2: Calculate Liturgical Calendar Context

Using the service date from the bulletin:

#### Liturgical Year (RCL Cycle)

| Year Letter | Primary Gospel | Years               |
| ----------- | -------------- | ------------------- |
| A           | Matthew        | 2026, 2029, 2032... |
| B           | Mark           | 2024, 2027, 2030... |
| C           | Luke           | 2025, 2028, 2031... |

The liturgical year begins on Advent I (late November/early December), NOT January 1.
Formula: `(calendar_year % 3)` → 0=C, 1=A, 2=B. But if the date falls before Advent I, use the prior year's calculation.

#### Liturgical Season

Determine the season and liturgical color:

| Season                          | Dates (approximate)              | Color                                     |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Advent                          | 4 Sundays before Christmas       | Purple/Blue                               |
| Christmas                       | Dec 25 - Jan 5                   | White/Gold                                |
| Epiphany                        | Jan 6 - day before Ash Wednesday | Green (White on Epiphany/Transfiguration) |
| Lent                            | Ash Wednesday - Holy Saturday    | Purple (Red on Palm Sunday)               |
| Holy Week                       | Palm Sunday - Holy Saturday      | Purple/Red                                |
| Easter                          | Easter Sunday - Pentecost Eve    | White/Gold                                |
| Pentecost Day                   | 50 days after Easter             | Red                                       |
| Ordinary Time (after Pentecost) | Day after Pentecost - Advent Eve | Green                                     |
| Special days                    | Varies                           | Red (martyrs, ordination), White (saints) |

#### RCL Readings

Look up the Revised Common Lectionary readings for this specific Sunday. Include:

- Old Testament reading
- Psalm
- Epistle reading
- Gospel reading

If you can't determine the exact RCL readings, note what the bulletin lists and flag it.

#### Narrative Lectionary Readings

Look up the Narrative Lectionary readings for this date. The NL uses a 4-year cycle:

| NL Year | Primary Gospel | Liturgical Years             |
| ------- | -------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Year 1  | Matthew        | 2022-23, 2026-27, 2030-31... |
| Year 2  | Mark           | 2023-24, 2027-28, 2031-32... |
| Year 3  | Luke           | 2024-25, 2028-29, 2032-33... |
| Year 4  | John           | 2025-26, 2029-30, 2033-34... |

The NL year runs September to May. Include:

- NL code (e.g., NL434)
- Title of the reading
- Primary reading
- Companion reading (if any)

Note whether the church's actual readings align with NL, RCL, both, or neither. Include a callout explaining the relationship.

#### Hebrew Calendar

Calculate or look up the approximate Hebrew date and note any Jewish observances that overlap.

#### Ecumenical Observances

Note any ecumenical observances for the date (Week of Prayer for Christian Unity, World Communion Sunday, etc.).
