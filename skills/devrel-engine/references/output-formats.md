# Output Format Templates

Universal templates for DevRel and community management deliverables. Adapt to specific product/community context from the conversation or project instructions. Always produce **two variants** of draft content.

---

## Framework Tags (Required on All Outputs)

Every content piece or task must carry these tags. This ensures recommendations are grounded in strategy, not just reactive.

```
JOURNEY STAGE: [Discover | Evaluate | Learn | Build | Scale]
DEVREL OBJECTIVE: [Awareness | Activation | Engagement | Innovation | Retention]
CONTENT PURPOSE: [Educational | Conversational | Showcasing | Operational]
TARGET ORBIT: [O4 Observers | O3 Participants | O2 Contributors | O1 Ambassadors]
```

## Community Discussion Prompt

Design for 10+ replies. Categorize by content purpose — Conversational prompts build relationships; Showcasing prompts provide recognition; Educational prompts transfer knowledge.

```
PLATFORM/CHANNEL: [Specific platform and channel/category]
TIMING: [Best day/time for the target audience]

PROMPT:
[Conversation starter — question or challenge, NOT an announcement.

Good patterns by content purpose:
  CONVERSATIONAL: "What's the hardest part about [common task in your domain]?"
  SHOWCASING: "Show us what you built/shipped this week — no matter how small!"
  EDUCATIONAL: "I just discovered you can [feature/technique] — has anyone tried this for [use case]?"
  CHALLENGE: "Build [specific thing] in under 30 minutes and share your result"

Avoid: Corporate announcements as questions, yes/no questions, jargon the audience
doesn't use, questions requiring expertise the audience doesn't have]

FOLLOW-UP 1 (after 3-5 replies):
[Build on responses — "Interesting that several of you mentioned [theme]..."]

FOLLOW-UP 2 (after discussion matures):
[Surface product feedback or showcase candidates — "Would anyone share their
project in a spotlight?" or "Should we add this to our feedback board?"]

ORBIT MOVEMENT GOAL: [e.g., "Pull O4→O3 by lowering barrier to first response"
or "Identify O2→O1 candidates for Champion recruitment"]

FRAMEWORK TAGS:
  Journey Stage: [stage]
  DevRel Objective: [Engagement / Activation]
  Content Purpose: [Conversational / Showcasing / Educational]
  Target Orbit: [level targeted]

SUCCESS: 10+ unique replies from non-staff members
```

## Social Post Drafts

Two variants per platform. Voice adapts to platform norms.

### X/Twitter
```
VARIANT A: [Max 280 chars. Punchy, conversational. Hooks, questions,
surprising stats, #BuildInPublic energy, user quotes. No corporate tone.]
VARIANT B: [Different angle on same topic]
HASHTAGS: [Relevant to topic and community]
MEDIA: [Screenshot, clip, or GIF suggestion]
FRAMEWORK TAGS: Journey: [stage] | Objective: [obj] | Purpose: [type] | Orbit: [level]
```

### LinkedIn
```
VARIANT A: [Professional, value-driven. Hook → context (2-3 sentences) → insight → CTA.
Business outcomes, success stories, industry trends, lessons learned.]
VARIANT B: [Different angle]
MEDIA: [Image, carousel, or video suggestion]
FRAMEWORK TAGS: Journey: [stage] | Objective: [obj] | Purpose: [type] | Orbit: [level]
```

### Reddit
```
SUBREDDIT: r/[specific sub]
VARIANT A: [Authentic, value-first. No self-promotion in opening. Genuine value.
Mention product naturally if relevant, never as the point.
Good: "I've been testing [category] tools for [use case] and here's what I found..."

VARIANT B: [Different framing]
COMMENT PREP: [2-3 responses for likely replies/questions]
FRAMEWORK TAGS: Journey: Discover/Evaluate | Objective: Awareness | Purpose: Conversational
```

### Dev.to / Hashnode / Technical Blog
```
TITLE: [Tutorial-style, search-optimized]
VARIANT A: [Outline — intro hook, problem statement, solution walkthrough, result, CTA]
VARIANT B: [Different angle or format]
FRAMEWORK TAGS: Journey: Evaluate/Learn | Objective: Activation | Purpose: Educational
```

## Community Support Response

Templates for common interaction types. Adapt voice to platform. Full set in `references/practitioner-craft.md`.

Use the appropriate pattern:
- **Bug report:** Acknowledge, reproduce, provide workaround, link to tracking issue, set expectations
- **Feature request:** Validate, link related issues, add to backlog with context, be honest about priority
- **Frustrated user:** Acknowledge specifically (not generically), provide solution or honest status, don't be defensive
- **First-time member:** Welcome warmly, answer directly, point to 2-3 resources, invite them to share their work
