SYNTHETIC FIXTURE (Fixture Co): student handout. The store, prices and sales are made up for practice.

# Student handout: run the numbers for the school store

The school store sells three items. Your job is to find which item makes the most profit, and to suggest one price change.

| Item | Price | Cost to us | Sold in week 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sticker | 2.50 | 1.00 | 40 |
| Notebook | 4.00 | 2.20 | 12 |
| Lanyard | 3.00 | 1.75 | 9 |

## Words you will use

- Markup: how much we add on top of cost, as a share of cost. (price - cost) / cost.
- Margin per item: price - cost.
- Gross profit: units sold times margin per item.
- Revenue: units sold times price.
- Query: a question you ask a table of data.

## Guided practice

1. Put each item on its own row. Prices go in column B, costs in column C, units in column E.
2. In D2, write a formula for the sticker's profit for the week. Use cell references, not typed numbers.
3. Predict the answer before you press Enter. Write your prediction down.
4. Do the same for the notebook and the lanyard rows.

## Independent application

1. In D5, add up the profit for all three items.
2. In F3, find the notebook's markup. Round it to four decimal places.
3. Change the notebook price to 4.50. Write one sentence: what changed in your sheet, and why?
4. Open the sales table (or the printed copy). Write one query for revenue by item and one for revenue by week. Copy the rows you get.
5. Which item brings in the most profit? Say how your numbers show it.

## Memo to the store manager

Write about 150 words. Start with your recommendation. Give your numbers as evidence. Name one risk of the change.

## No computer?

Use the printed table and a calculator. For the queries, make a tally table and write in words what your query would ask.
