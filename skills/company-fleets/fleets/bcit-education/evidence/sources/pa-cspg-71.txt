L156: CSPG 71 - Computer Science 7-12
L157: Modified: April 1, 2022
L159: Grade Level Scope of Certificate
L160 (paraphrase): A Pennsylvania Computer Science 7-12 certificate covers computer science courses in grades 7 to 12, and grade 6 assignments (see CSPG 104).
L162 (paraphrase): The certificate covers courses built on the CSTA K-12 standards: algorithms and programming, computing systems, data, impacts of computing and networks, plus related areas such as cybersecurity.
L166 (paraphrase): Educators teaching computer science in a Pennsylvania LEA on 2022-12-31 under the listed certificates may continue within their certificate's grade limits.
L171: Business Computer Information Technology PK-12
L173 (paraphrase): Anyone first teaching computer science in grades 7-12 in a Pennsylvania LEA after 2022-12-31, or moving to a new LEA after that date, needs the PDE computer science certificate.
