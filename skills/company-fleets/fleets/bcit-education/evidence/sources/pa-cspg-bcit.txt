L156: ​CSPG 33 - Business/Computer and Information Technology, PK-12
L157: ​Instructional Area Code 1603
L161 (paraphrase): A Pennsylvania Business/Computer and Information Technology certificate covers business and basic computer and IT courses in grades PK-12.
L163 (paraphrase): BCIT-certified educators may teach software application and business software courses, such as keyboarding, word processing, databases, spreadsheets and desktop publishing.
L182 (paraphrase): Per CSPG 71, BCIT-certified educators teaching computer science in a Pennsylvania LEA on 2022-12-31 may continue; a first computer science assignment in grades 7-12 after that date needs the computer science certificate.
