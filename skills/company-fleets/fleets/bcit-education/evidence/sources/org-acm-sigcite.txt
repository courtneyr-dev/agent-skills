L74: SIGCITE is the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) Special Interest Group on Cybersecurity & Information Technology Education (formerly, SIGITE). Our members include cybersecurity and information technology faculty (teachers and researchers), students, and industry professionals.
L75: With over 400 members worldwide, SIGCITE drives the creation and dissemination of the computing discipline of information technology, including cybersecurity. The organization has created a model undergraduate curriculum and helped create accreditation guidelines for IT programs, and is now defining and promoting IT and Security research.
