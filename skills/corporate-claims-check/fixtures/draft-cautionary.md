# What to expect from an SEO engagement

Google guarantees nothing about rankings. Anyone who promises a #1 ranking is guessing. Agencies that promise top rankings should worry you.

Our Google-certified team audits your site's visibility. We hit our deadline and listed your top 10 keywords.

The report lists the pages that reach the top 3 results. Within 30 days we audit every page and show which pages sit in the top 10.

The theme's warranty covers bugs, not rankings.

Google advises: "If they guarantee you that their changes will give you first place in search results, find someone else."[^S1]
