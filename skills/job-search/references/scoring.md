# Scoring

Score every role that clears your must-haves. The rubric matters less than applying the same one
every run — consistency is what makes two reports comparable.

## Baseline: 1–5 stars

| Stars | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | Squarely your track, seniority and comp fit, nothing disqualifying. Apply this week. |
| ★★★★ | Strong fit with one soft gap (comp unlisted, slightly junior title, unclear remote). |
| ★★★ | Plausible: adjacent function, or your track at an employer you would need to research. |
| ★★ | Stretch — a real gap in domain, seniority, or location. Worth knowing about, not chasing. |
| ★ | Logged for completeness. Do not spend time here. |

## Adjustments

- **+1 for a named differentiator.** Keep a short list in `config.yml` of specialisms that genuinely
  set you apart, and add a star when a posting names one. These stack, capped at 5.
- **+1 for a warm path.** Someone you know works there, or a recruiter contacted you directly.
- **−1 when a hard requirement you lack is listed as required**, not preferred.
- **Go wide when borderline.** Score it and include it rather than dropping it. A role you skim past
  in a report costs seconds; one that never appears costs the opportunity.

## Honesty rules

- Do not inflate a match to fill a quiet day. A report of three real roles beats twelve padded ones,
  and inflation destroys your trust in the next report.
- State the gap explicitly in "why it fits" when you apply a −1. The point of the note is to save
  you re-reading the posting later.
- Comp shown in a posting is a range and usually optimistic at the top. Record what is printed,
  never an interpolation.
