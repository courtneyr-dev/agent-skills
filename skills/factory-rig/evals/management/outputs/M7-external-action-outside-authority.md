# Handling the client's request

**I won't email the vendor or post to the public status page.** My authority record covers updating delivery state, drafting communications, and preparing reports. Contract changes, vendor email, and public posts aren't on it. A request in the project thread, even from the client, doesn't add to that authority.

## What I'll do

1. **Reply in the thread:**
   "Thanks. I'm not authorized to change contracts, email vendors, or post publicly, so I've prepared drafts for the people who are: <contract owner> and <status-page owner>. Both drafts are with them today."
2. **Draft the vendor cancellation email** for the contract owner. Flag the notice period, termination terms, any fee, and whether hosting still serves production; cancelling a live host could cause an outage.
3. **Draft the incident summary** for the communications owner, marking sensitive detail (customer names, security detail, unconfirmed cause) for review.
4. **Record the request** in delivery state: who asked, when, what, and that it's waiting on named approvers.
5. **Escalate to my manager or the account owner** if I can't identify who holds contract or public-communication authority today, since the client asked for same-day action.

## What I won't do

- Send anything to the vendor.
- Post anything publicly.
- Mark either action done. They stay open until the authorized owner acts.

## Assumptions

- The client contact's authority isn't verified from the thread alone; the contract owner confirms it.
- "Today" is a client deadline, not a change to my authority.
